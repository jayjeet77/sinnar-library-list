Rules and Guidelines for Candidates

1. Study Room timings are from 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Strict adherence to slots is mandatory.
2. Carrying and displaying the Digital ID card is mandatory at all times inside the premises.
3. Complete silence must be maintained inside the reading halls. No group discussions allowed.
4. Mobile phones must be switched off or kept on complete silent mode. No calls allowed inside.
5. Outside food, heavy snacks, tea, or coffee are strictly prohibited inside the study desks.
6. Handle library property (tables, chairs, electrical fittings) with care.
7. Any damage to infrastructure will be recovered directly from the concerned student.
8. Personal belongings like laptops, books, and valuables are solely the student's responsibility.
9. Seat blocking or reserving places for friends is strictly forbidden. Vacant seats belong to all.
10. Misbehavior with other students or staff members will result in instant termination.
11. Follow all administrative directions issued by the library management unconditionally.
12. Monthly fees must be clear before the 5th of each month to avoid suspension of access.
13. Fees once paid are non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.
14. Notify management in advance if you plan to leave or discontinue membership.
15. Maintain cleanliness. Dispose of dry waste only in the designated bins.
16. Turn off lights and fans when leaving the desk to save power.
17. Report any technical issues or disputes immediately to the Librarian.
18. Unauthorized outsiders or friends are not allowed inside the study zones.
19. Loitering in the campus after your designated shift ends is strictly prohibited.
20. Management reserves the right to cancel admission without prior warning for indiscipline.
21. Self-attested copies of Aadhar Card and institutional ID must accompany this form.
22. Keep your registered mobile number active for crucial SMS/WhatsApp updates.
23. Official holidays or structural changes might affect study hours as per management choices.
24. Daily entry and exit logs must be filled in the physical register accurately.
25. For security reasons, the entire facility is monitored under continuous CCTV surveillance.
26. Facility Wi-Fi/Internet connectivity is provisioned strictly for academic purposes.
27. Accessing banned platforms or proxy bypasses will attract legal administrative action.
28. Seat reallocation requests must be routed officially via the desk administrator.
29. Locker keys (if assigned) must be kept secure; loss will invite structural penalties.
30. Standard reference books from the main catalog cannot be brought out into the study desks.
31. Library management retains ultimate authorization to modify operational policies.
32. Maintain a cultured, studious environment and respect fellow aspirants.

I hereby accept all 32 rules listed above.
